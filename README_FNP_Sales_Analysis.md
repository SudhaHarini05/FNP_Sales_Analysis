# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from **Ferns N Petals (FNP)** to understand revenue performance, customer spending, product performance, order patterns, and delivery efficiency. An interactive Excel dashboard summarizes key metrics and visualizes trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on the following business questions:

- What is the overall revenue generated?
- What are the average order value and order-to-delivery time?
- How does revenue vary month by month?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which occasions contribute the most revenue?
- Which cities have the highest number of orders?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How can product popularity by occasion and order quantity be used to improve business decisions?

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — data analysis, PivotTables, charts, dashboard creation, and slicers/timeline filters
- **Data visualization** — column charts, line charts, KPI cards, and interactive filters

## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard presents:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders Placed
- Average Order-to-Delivery Time
- Average Customer Spend / Average Order Value
- Revenue by Occasion
- Top 10 Cities by Orders
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top 5 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Order Hour
- Monthly Revenue
- Interactive filters for occasion, order date, and delivery date

## Key Findings

Based on the displayed dashboard:

- **Total revenue:** ₹35,20,984.00
- **Total orders:** 1,000
- **Average customer spend / average order value:** ₹3,520.98
- **Average order-to-delivery time:** 5.53 (confirm the unit before reporting)
- **Revenue by occasion:** Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan appear among the strongest revenue-generating occasions in the displayed chart.
- **Revenue by category:** Soft Toys is the highest-revenue category in the displayed chart, followed by Sweets and Colors.
- **Top product:** Magam Set is the highest-revenue product among the five products displayed.
- **City orders:** Kavali has the highest order count among the cities displayed.
- **Monthly performance:** Revenue fluctuates across the year; February appears to be the strongest month in the displayed chart.

These observations should be validated against the source data and PivotTable calculations before being used in formal business reporting.

## Business Recommendations

1. Plan occasion-specific promotions around high-performing occasions.
2. Review inventory and availability for high-revenue products and categories.
3. Investigate weaker months to understand whether sales changes relate to demand, product mix, pricing, or promotions.
4. Explore targeted marketing in cities with promising order activity.
5. Monitor delivery time and investigate factors that may contribute to delays.
6. Analyze product popularity by occasion and compare order quantity with delivery time to improve campaign planning and fulfillment.

## How to Use

2. Open the Excel workbook in Microsoft Excel.
3. Open the dashboard worksheet.
4. Use the Occasion slicer and Order Date / Delivery Date timelines to filter the dashboard.
5. Review the KPI cards and charts to explore changes in sales performance.
